## Data used by the app

NaviGo uses precise device location while the app is in use to show the user's position, find nearby places, calculate routes and road distances, follow route progress, detect rerouting, and determine arrival. The app also stores saved places, recent place selections, offline-map metadata, and downloaded offline map data on the device.

The current version does not require an account and does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking code. [CONFIRM THIS REMAINS TRUE BEFORE PUBLISHING.]
